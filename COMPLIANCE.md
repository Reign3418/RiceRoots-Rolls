## 2. Major Food Allergen Transparency (FDA FALCPA & FASTER Act)

Our culinary team prepares authentic Cambodian, Chinese, and Japanese sushi dishes using shared kitchen equipment:
- **Peanuts & Tree Nuts:** Used in our `Chicken or Pork Noodle Bowl` and select Southeast Asian sauces.
- **Sesame:** Used in our `Sesame Chicken`, `Sushi Nachos` (furikake), toasted sesame garnishes, and sesame oil.
- **Shellfish & Finfish:** Used across our `Kathiew (Cambodian Pho)`, `Shrimp Tempura`, `Crab Rangoon`, `Trey Chein`, and sushi bar rolls.
- **Soy, Wheat & Egg:** Present in house soy glazes, tempura batters, egg rolls, gyoza wrappers, and fried rice.

Guests with food allergies are encouraged to call `(910) 920-1159` directly when placing takeout or catering orders.
