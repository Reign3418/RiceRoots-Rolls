## 🌾 Overview: Where Passion Meets Function

Built for **Rice, Roots & Rolls – An Asian Kitchen** at **1124 N Bragg Blvd, Spring Lake, NC 28390** (`(910) 920-1159`), honoring Chef A's journey from her red **Far East Foodie** food truck in Lander, Wyoming to her brick-and-mortar restaurant and sushi bar near Fort Bragg, NC—and designed around her life raising two young boys with a 45-minute daily commute.

### Key Capabilities Built Into This Platform:
1. **3 Switchable Cultural Site Concepts (Zero Neon Colors):**
   - **🌿 Concept 1 — Angkor Sanctuary & Teak (`sanctuary`):** Inspired by her serene botanical dining room (`dining-room-interior.jpg`) and Ta Prohm temple roots (`story-roots.jpg`). Deep espresso teak, botanical forest sage, burnished Angkor gold, and subtle rising pho steam.
   - **🌾 Concept 2 — Jasmine & Silk Heritage (`jasmine`):** Warm steamed rice paper (`#FAF6EE`), Khmer terracotta (`#8C3B2B`), and sunlit editorial storytelling highlighting her two boys and family recipes.
   - **🍣 Concept 3 — Bragg Blvd Sushi & Craft Board (`craftboard`):** High-contrast Nori Slate side-by-side Main Kitchen Board + Hand-Rolled Sushi Bar Board, plus a gallery of her original printable menu boards and verified 5-star reviews.
2. **"Never Miss the Turn" N Bragg Blvd Wayfinding Hub:**
   - Locked to exact rooftop coordinates (`35.1776006, -78.9795992` / `https://maps.app.goo.gl/CQzkFrkVHxttnsVk6`) with 1-tap buttons for **iPhone Apple Maps**, **Google Maps**, and **Waze**, plus Northbound and Southbound lane cues.
3. **iPhone-First "On-the-Fly" Pocket Admin (`ChefAdminModal.jsx`):**
   - Built for Chef A's 45-minute commute and family schedule: 1-tap presets for **Normal Hours**, **45-Min Commute Traffic Delay**, **Boys' Family/School Event (Early Close)**, **Catering Prep Mode**, and **Fast 86 Sold-Out Toggles**, plus a **1-Tap Google Business Profile Sync Bridge**.
4. **Full `Schema.org` Restaurant + 36-Item Menu Microdata + White Paper (`SchemaDiscoveryWhitePage.jsx`):**
   - Replaces Canva's unindexed flat PNG menu images with 36 fully searchable dishes and prices in JSON-LD + interactive SEO & Google Maps sync brief.
5. **1080p Dining Room TV Menu & USB Exporter (`TvMenuModal.jsx`):**
   - Fixed `1920 × 1080` digital menu board that syncs live when items are 86'd or exports a crisp 1080p JPG for a USB thumb drive.
